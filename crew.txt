The crew of the Starship Horizon had been drifting through the Andromeda galaxy for months. 
Captain Marcus Webb, at 52 years old, had seen more than any human should ever witness. 
His first officer, Lieutenant Sara Chen, 34, often questioned his decisions but trusted his instincts. 
Together, they navigated the unknown, their only guide the faint pulse of a dying star.

On the research station Kepler-12, Dr. Thomas Richter, 47, and his assistant, Dr. Maya Patel, 29, 
discovered a microscopic organism that defied all known laws of biology. 
The creature, which they named "Astra," seemed to communicate through light patterns. 
Maya spent sleepless nights decoding its signals, while Thomas documented every finding with obsessive precision.

Meanwhile, the mining vessel Red Dwarf, commanded by Captain Elena Rossi, 41, 
and her partner, Commander James Okafor, 38, stumbled upon a derelict alien ship. 
Inside, they found frozen specimens of an unknown humanoid species.
